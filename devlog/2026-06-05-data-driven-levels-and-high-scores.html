Update — Data-Driven Levels & Saved High Scores

A small update focused on polish and future content. No new levels yet, but the groundwork is in to make new ones much faster to build.

What's new

  • Levels are now data-driven. Spawn points, timers, goals and enemy waves are loaded from external files, so levels can be tuned and expanded without rebuilding the game.
  • High score now saves. Your best score is stored locally and carries over between sessions, so you can keep chasing it.

Thanks for playing — and good luck topping the leaderboard!
